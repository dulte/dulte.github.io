How this page works

This page transfers files peer-to-peer using WebRTC data channels with copy/paste signaling.

  • Sender: choose a file, copy the generated SDP and share it with the receiver.
  • Receiver: paste the sender's SDP, copy the receiver's SDP back to the sender.
  • After the sender pastes the receiver answer, the transfer will start. The receiver will be prompted to download the received file.
  • Transfers are encrypted (AES-GCM) and a SHA‑256 checksum is used to verify integrity.
  • Persistent RSA keys are stored in your browser to simplify later transfers.

The sender can pause, resume, or cancel while sending. ETA and progress are shown.
